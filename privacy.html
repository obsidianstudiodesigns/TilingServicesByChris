What we collect

We only collect the information you choose to give us, such as:

  • your name, phone number and email address
  • your address or suburb, so we can visit the site and do the work
  • details, measurements and photos of the room or area to be tiled or painted
  • billing details when we invoice you for work

How the quote form works

The quote form on this website does not store or send anything to us by itself. When you press "Send on WhatsApp" or "Send by email", it opens your own WhatsApp or email app with a message already written. Nothing reaches us unless you send that message yourself.

Why we use it

  • to reply to your enquiry and give you a quote
  • to arrange site visits and carry out the work
  • to send invoices and keep records we are required to keep by law
  • to contact you about follow-up work you have asked for

We do not sell, rent or trade your personal information, and we do not send marketing unless you ask for it.

Who we share it with

We only share your information when we need to in order to do the job, or when the law requires it. The services we use to talk to you, such as WhatsApp and Facebook (Meta) and our email provider (Google), process messages under their own privacy policies.

This website, cookies and third parties

This website does not use cookies, analytics or advertising trackers. To load, your browser fetches files from these providers, which may record your IP address and browser details in their standard server logs:

  • the company that hosts this website
  • Google Fonts, which supplies the typefaces
  • jsDelivr, which supplies the 3D graphics library

Links to Facebook, WhatsApp, Google Maps and other sites take you to services with their own privacy policies.

Photos of our work

Photos on this website show work we have done. We do not publish street addresses with them. If a photo shows your home and you would like it removed, contact us and we will take it down.

How long we keep it

We keep enquiry messages only as long as we need them to deal with your request. Records of work and invoices are kept for as long as tax and other laws require, usually five years, and then deleted.

How we protect it

We take reasonable steps to keep your information safe, including keeping it on password-protected devices and accounts and only sharing it when needed.

Your rights

Under POPIA you may:

  • ask what personal information we hold about you
  • ask us to correct or delete it
  • object to us using it
  • withdraw consent you have given

Contact us using the details above and we will respond within a reasonable time. If you are unhappy with how we handle your information, you may complain to the Information Regulator of South Africa at inforegulator.org.za.

Children

Our services are for adults. We do not knowingly collect information from children.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The date at the top shows when it last changed.
