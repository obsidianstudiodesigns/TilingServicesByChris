How a room gets retiled

Scroll through one job from start to finish. The room on screen is stripped, prepared, waterproofed, tiled and painted, the same order Chris works in on site.

  1. 1

    Lift the old tiles

    Old tiles come up and the rubble is carted away. Nothing new goes down over loose, hollow or cracked tiles.

  2. 2

    Prepare the surface

    The base is cleaned and made sound and flat. A good base is what stops new tiles cracking or lifting later.

  3. 3

    Waterproof the shower

    Shower walls get a waterproofing membrane before a single tile goes on, so water stays in the shower and out of the wall.

  4. 4

    Set out and lay

    Patterns and inlays are set out first. Adhesive is combed on with a notched trowel and every tile is set level, with spacers keeping the joints even.

  5. 5

    Grout, clean and paint

    Joints are grouted and the tiles cleaned off. The walls can be painted in the same job, so the room is finished, not just tiled.
